For parents

Fungi! · Mendebur Studios · Last updated: 20 November 2026

Fungi! is a free family game that brings the classic hide-and-seek of our childhood into a forest. This page answers the questions parents ask most.

QuestionAnswer
Are there ads?No ad network. Between rounds, trailers of the same developer’s other games may occasionally appear; the store button sits behind a parental gate.
Is there chat?No. There is no voice or text chat.
Can my child play with strangers?“Play Now” may match them with other players looking for a game; empty places are filled with computer players. With no chat, players see each other only as a character and a nickname. To play only with people you know, use “Play with Friends” and the 4-letter room code.
Leaderboards?Best round scores are shown with a nickname and a 3-digit tag. In Settings you can hide from the leaderboards or delete the entries.
What data do you collect?Anonymous total counts, and room data only while a room is open. Details in the Privacy Policy.
Any purchases?No, the game is completely free.
Are nicknames safe?They are optional; names with swear words, phone numbers, emails or links are rejected. Remind your child not to use their real full name.
Mushrooms?Every Mushroom Hunt starts by reminding children never to touch real mushrooms without an adult. The game is not a mushroom guide.

How do I delete data?

On-device data is removed when you delete the app or clear browser data. Room data is deleted automatically when the room closes. For any other request email info@mindsharp.co; we reply within 30 days.
